Introduction

Every family has recipes that beg to be shared. Voss Kitchen is a place for family and friends to record and share their favorite recipes so they are handy to pass along... or even just to recall ingredients while shopping.

How it works

Voss Kitchen is open for everyone to browse with no account required. Adding and managing recipes requires an account and is reserved for approved family members and friends - by invite only.

Onboarding Process

  1. Receive an invitation to join Voss Kitchen.
  2. Open the invitation and visit your account page.
  3. Set your password using the Change Password option.
  4. Wait for an administrator to approve your account.
  5. Once approved, log in and start adding recipes.

Until your account is approved, you can still browse the cookbook like everyone else.
